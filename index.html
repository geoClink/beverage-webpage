COMMONLY ASKED // FAQ
What does "liquid pressure extraction" mean?
We use a mechanical cold-press pressure system rather than chemical solvents like hexane or ethanol. It preserves the natural botanical profile without leaving synthetic residue.
When does nationwide shipping begin?
Our e-commerce portal and nationwide logistics officially launch on October 15. Until then, we are exclusively fulfilling local pickups and priority phone orders.
Do the beverages need to be refrigerated?
Our Batch 01 local glass bottles are fresh and should be kept cold. Our upcoming 12oz cans (launching Oct 15) are shelf-stable and can be stored at ambient temperatures.
