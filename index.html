FOUNDER & ORIGIN STORY

From Plant Science Roots to Uncompromising Purity in Traverse City.

Our path started in the field: rooted in rigorous agricultural science and hands-on botanical research, with a commitment to pure, uncompromised cultivation standards.

That deep scientific foundation planted a strict refusal to rely on chemical shortcuts or synthetic isolates. Botanical Archive was built out of that exact conviction—operating locally with active state licensing in Traverse City, using pure liquid pressure extraction to secure the true plant profile, and laying the groundwork toward an independent natural water source.

Knowledge Base & FAQ
Q1 ZERO SOLVENTS

What does "liquid pressure extraction" mean?

We use a mechanical cold-press pressure system rather than chemical solvents like hexane or ethanol. It preserves the natural botanical profile without leaving synthetic residue.

Q2 SHIPPING

When does nationwide shipping begin?

Our e-commerce portal and nationwide logistics officially launch on October 15. Until then, we are exclusively fulfilling local pickups and priority phone orders.

Q3 STORAGE

Do the beverages need to be refrigerated?

Our local glass bottles are fresh and should be kept cold. Our upcoming 12oz cans (launching October 15) are shelf-stable and can be stored at ambient temperatures.

Q4 PARTNERSHIPS

How do I join the partnership or draft keg project?

Submit your venue or hospitality details via our reservation form or contact our direct phone line. We review partner allocations weekly to supply draft kegs and dedicated mixers.
